Featured Astronomical Terms

Nebula

Interstellar Medium

A vast cloud of gas and dust in space, often acting as stellar nurseries or the remains of exploded stars.

Kepler-186f

Exoplanetary Science

Kepler-186f is the first Earth-size exoplanet discovered within the habitable zone of another star, located roughly 500 light-years away.

Black Hole

Relativistic Astrophysics

A region of spacetime where gravity is so intense that nothing — not even light — can escape its gravitational pull.
